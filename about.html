🎒

General SEN support

I provide specialised educational support across the key stages, shaped around each learner's needs.

  • Age and stages: Children and young people aged 5 to 18, from KS1 to KS5.
  • Learning differences: SpLD, dyslexia, dyscalculia, dyspraxia, processing and memory issues, ADD, ADHD and autism.
  • Additional needs: Visual impairments, speech and language difficulties, emotional difficulties, Global Developmental Delay and Cerebral Palsy.
  • Core learning: Communication, phonics, reading, spelling, literacy and numeracy.
  • Subjects and study: English, Mathematics and Science, alongside revision planning, study skills and strategies for effective learning.
🏫

SEN teaching background

My 15 years of experience span different age groups, school settings and levels of need.

  • School settings: Primary and secondary education in mainstream and specialised institutions.
  • Specialist training: SEN approaches for dyslexia, dyspraxia, dyscalculia, ADHD, and speech and language impairments.
  • Professional teamwork: Close collaboration with educational psychologists, occupational therapists, speech and language therapists, and other educational experts.
🤝

Parental engagement

Parental involvement is central to effective, joined-up support.

  • Open communication: Families are kept involved through clear, ongoing conversations.
  • Shared decisions: Planning is collaborative and shaped with parents.
  • Teaching quality: My teaching has been recognised as good or outstanding in various assessments.
